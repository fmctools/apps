A Google Maps-inspired navigation app for the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2 that brings your route, nearby streets, and turn-by-turn directions directly to your wrist over Bluetooth.

The live map fills the screen and smoothly tracks your GPS movement with phone orientation in real time. You can switch between two map orientations: one where the center indicator rotates to match your direction while the map stays fixed, and another where the entire map rotates around you as you move. You can also save Home and Work locations for quick access.

## Requirements

- The [patched firmware](/firmware) (`fw-wp2.bin` from 2026-11-13 or later) and the [App Loader](/firmware)(2026-11-13 or later).

- Vela for Android with the watch link, [APK on GitHub](https://github.com/abdalrahmanmamdouh2004-svg/V-Maps/releases/tag/V-Maps), on the phone that is paired with the watch in Nothing X. In

- Vela: Settings > Nav device > CMF watch test, pick the watch, turn on **Show navigation on the watch**.
