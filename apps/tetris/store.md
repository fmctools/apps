Falling blocks on your wrist. Pieces drop into a 10 by 20 well: fill a row and it clears. Clear ten rows and the
pieces fall faster. A faint outline shows where the piece will land. Your best score is kept on the watch.

- **Turn the crown** to move the piece left and right.
- **Tap** the screen to turn it.
- **Hold** your finger on the screen to drop it faster.

Press the crown or swipe right to leave.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware). Open it from the **Apps** row at the end of the
watch's app list (list style, not the waterfall).
