Crack a steel safe using the rotating crown and tactile vibration feedback on your wrist.

- **Classic:** Endless streak of safes with 3 lives. Alternate turn directions for each pin, feel the lock click when you hit the slot, and crack open the vault door to claim the gold inside.
- **Heist (10s–60s):** Pick your time limit (`10s`, `20s`, `30s`, `40s`, `50s`, or `60s`), fly into the bank, and crack the safe before the police sirens sound in the final 5 seconds. Win to see the door swing open with gold; run out of time and land behind bars.
- **Blitz (60s):** Endless speed arcade. Spin either way to crack as many single-pin locks as possible before time runs out.

Best scores and fastest Heist clear times are saved on the watch and shown on the optional widget card left of the watch face.

Press the crown or swipe right to exit.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware). Open it from the **Apps** row at the end of the watch's app list (list style, not waterfall).
