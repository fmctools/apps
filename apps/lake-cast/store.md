Stand on the sunny sandy beach, cast your rod anywhere onto the open water ahead, and battle fish on the vertical catch bar.

- **Cast anywhere on the water:** Tap shallow shore water or deep blue water ahead to swing your rod from the sand and land your bobber right there.
- **Live fish underwater:** Watch 6 distinct fish species glide across the lake, cast near them, and react to the nibble tap and `HOOK!` splash alert.
- **Vertical bar duel:** When a fish bites, use the crown up and down to keep it inside the green catch zone on the left bar as it darts up and down.
- **Modes, Difficulty & Trophies:** Choose **Easy** (classic gentle bar), **Medium** (`50/50` balanced pace), or **Hard** (fast fish, narrow bar) before starting **Relax** or **Derby 90s**, and unlock all 6 species in **Trophies** with weight records synced to the widget card.

Press the crown or swipe right to exit.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware). Open it from the **Apps** row at the end of the watch's app list (list style, not waterfall).
