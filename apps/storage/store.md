Storage shows the writable space available on the watch, with a segmented usage bar and sizes for installed
apps, the App Loader, watch faces and other files. System storage lists firmware and resource reservations
separately from free file space.

Tap a category for details. The crown scrolls, the crown button goes back, and Refresh scans again. The app is
read-only: it does not delete files, remove apps or change firmware. File sizes are logical lengths;
filesystem allocation and metadata can make occupied space larger. Incomplete scans are labelled rather than
presented as complete totals.

## Requirements

CMF Watch Pro 2 running patched firmware 1.0.0.73 and an App Loader providing
storage-inspection service v1. On an older loader, the app displays an update message. No phone connection is
needed. Watch 3 Pro support has not been established.
