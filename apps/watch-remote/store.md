Watch Remote turns your watch into a remote for your laptop, PC or Google TV. The watch talks to your phone, and
your phone shows up on the PC or TV as a Bluetooth keyboard and media remote. No app is needed on the PC or TV.

**Page 1** is a D-pad: up, down, left and right, with OK (Enter) in the middle. Use it to move around menus,
slides or a TV home screen. Hold a button and it keeps going, like holding a key on a keyboard.

**Page 2** has Back, Home, Previous, Play/Pause, Next, Mute and Volume. Tap the speaker icon and turn the crown to
change the volume. It switches off by itself after a second.

Turn the crown or tap the dots at the bottom to switch pages. Press the crown to leave.

You can change what every button does in the phone app.

## Requirements

- The **Watch Menu** phone app (Android 9 or newer), from
  [github.com/abdalrahmanmamdouh2004-svg/replies](https://github.com/abdalrahmanmamdouh2004-svg/replies).
  Turn on Watch Remote there, then pair your PC or TV with the phone over Bluetooth once.
- The watch paired with the **Nothing X** app first, then connected in Watch Menu.
- The patched firmware with the channel key (2026-11-13 or later) and the App Loader.
